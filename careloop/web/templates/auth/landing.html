How it works

  1. At each dose time you get a reminder. Press "I took it" when you have.
  2. If nobody answers, CareLoop tells your caretaker, then your family, at times you choose.
  3. Helpers can suggest changes to medicines or appointments. Nothing changes unless you approve.
  4. Everything is recorded, so everyone sees the same honest history.
